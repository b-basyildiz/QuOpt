Continuous-pulse SGD step-size choice (2026-10-14)

Recommendation: use h=0.0005 for optimization in the current QOC.bash setup.
Re-evaluate final optimized weights at h=0.00025 without changing the weights.
If finer accuracy is needed, continue refinement to h=0.000125 or below.
This final verification is a recommendation, not an automatic pipeline stage.

Setup: iSWAP, SpeedUp, g=1, 40 sin^2 pulses, cap=40, anharmonicity=14,
staggering=17, qutrit with leakage, crosstalk, CFME4, no leakage penalty.
T/Tmin ranges from 0.1 to 1, with Tmin=pi/2. Pulse duration T/40 ranges
from 0.00392699 to 0.0392699. h=0.0005 yields 8-79 integration steps,
or 16-158 distinct Gauss quadrature times, per pulse. The controls remain
40 x 8 = 320 trainable angles regardless of h. SGD starts with lr=0.3,
momentum=0.99, Nesterov=True, and the existing ReduceLROnPlateau scheduler.

Prior frozen-control sweep (continuous_steps_2026-10-12):
h          steps/pulse    maximum absolute fidelity error
0.001      4-40           8.21925e-6
0.0005     8-79           5.08782e-7
0.000125   32-315         2.43892e-9
These are empirical maxima across 50 starting-control seed slots plus two
stress controls at each of 10 durations. They are not global error bounds.
The previous h=0.000125 choice required |delta F| <= 1e-8 AND
||delta U||_F/4 <= 1e-6. The new choice relaxes that strict propagation
criterion to prioritize optimization cost; it does not pass the old limits.

New production-code checks:
All runs call actual ML.fidelity_ml and its unchanged SGD/scheduler.
Same initial controls for every h: frozen random_slot_00_seed_628.
12 iterations at normalized T=0.1, 3 at T=0.5, 1 at T=1.0 for all h.
At T=1.0 this is a loss/gradient/runtime check of the initial controls,

The evidence supports cheaper optimization with essentially unchanged early
gradients at h=0.0005. It does not show that smaller h intrinsically suppresses
SGD gradients, nor establish convergence of the full 5000-iteration, 50-seed
production sweep. An h change affects integration, not the SGD learning rate.

Files: h*.json contain complete histories and provenance; h*.npz contain
initial angles, first gradients and best angles. shortest_100/ contains the
longer pilots and final fixed-weight refinement. comparison.json summarizes
gradient comparisons and the selection. No production Data/ files were written.
